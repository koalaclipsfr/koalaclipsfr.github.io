Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The KoalaClipsFR Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the KoalaClipsFR YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to upload videos and read or update the video details (title, description, tags, privacy and schedule) of the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are used only to upload and manage KoalaClipsFR videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from viewers or any other users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
